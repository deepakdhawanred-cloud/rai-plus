Fission
  • Heavy nucleus splits (e.g. ²³⁵U + slow n)
  • Products: two medium nuclei + 2–3 neutrons
  • ≈ 200 MeV per event (≈ 0.85 MeV per nucleon)
  • Starts at ordinary temperatures
  • Chain reaction; controlled in reactors
  • Radioactive waste
  • Used today for electricity
Fusion
  • Light nuclei join (e.g. ²H + ³H)
  • Products: heavier nucleus (+ neutron)
  • 17.6 MeV per D–T event (≈ 3.5 MeV per nucleon)
  • Needs ≈ 10⁷ K and high pressure
  • Powers the Sun and stars
  • Much less long-lived waste
  • Controlled fusion still experimental

Both move nuclei towards the peak of the binding-energy-per-nucleon curve near A = 56, so both release energy.
